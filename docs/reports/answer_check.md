## q070: cross_reference, english (in scope, answered)

```text
What notice must a new owner give before evicting a tenant whose lease does not bind him?
```

```text
Article 605 | المادة ٦٠٥ states that a person acquiring a leased property who is not bound by the lease can only evict the lessee by giving him notice as provided for in Article 563. Article 563 specifies the notice periods depending on the term of the lease. Article 598 | المادة ٥٩٨ states that a lease ends at the expiration of the agreed term without it being necessary to give notice of evacuation. Article 600 | المادة ٦٠٠ clarifies that if notice of evacuation has been given and the lessee continues to enjoy the property after the expiration of the lease, the lease will not be deemed renewed unless there is proof to the contrary. Article 608 | المادة ٦٠٨ does not directly address the notice requirement for eviction. Therefore, the notice required before evicting a tenant whose lease does not bind the new owner is as provided in Article 563.
```
