Answer:

The owner of a thing has, within the limits of the law, the right to use, enjoy, and dispose of it (`Article 802`). This includes owning all essential elements of the thing owned (`Article 803`). The state cannot take property from the owner unless the owner's rights are endangered and they fail to provide security or continue to use the property unlawfully (`Article 988`). In such cases, the judge may take the property and entrust it to a third party for management, or declare the usufruct extinguished (`Article 988`). ```
