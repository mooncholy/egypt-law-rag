# Optimization: AWQ and a distilled reranker (Phase 13)

Two changes, each measured against the baseline on fixed question sets, then together:

- **AWQ:** the generator `Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` in 4 bits (`Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-AWQ`, served by vLLM) against the 8-bit baseline (`qwen2.5:7b-instruct-q8_0`, served by Ollama).
- **Distilled reranker:** a 118M-parameter multilingual cross-encoder trained on `bge-reranker-v2-m3`'s scores (568M), against the teacher (`docs/reports/distillation.md`).

Hardware for every row: one NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Ti (16 GB) under WSL 2.

**Why each worse number is worse.**
- **AWQ, faithfulness 0.868 → 0.765.** The 65-question means aren't comparable as they stand: the judge left 21 of AWQ's 57 answers unscored, against none of q8's. On the 36 questions both runs scored, the drop is 0.817 → 0.765 (−0.052). The 4-bit answers are longer (150 tokens on average, 127 for q8), and some add claims the articles don't make (q077). Three drift into Chinese (q015, q045, q064); q8's q064 answer does too.
- **AWQ, time to first token 111 ms → 489 ms.** It's measured, but the cause isn't isolated. This row changes the backend (Ollama to vLLM) as well as the weights.
- **AWQ, VRAM 7.83 → 8.65 GiB.** vLLM reserves 60% of the card up front, KV cache included: 5.29 GiB of weights, 0.28 GiB of CUDA graphs and 1.96 GiB of KV cache (36,608 tokens). Ollama loads the 8-bit weights (8.1 GB on disk) with its own 8,192-token context. The 4-bit weights are smaller, but this serving setup holds more memory.
- **Distilled reranker, recall@5 0.840 → 0.733 and CP@5 0.820 → 0.673.** The 118M student loses 14 of the 131 in-scope questions. Training it on the teacher's scores didn't beat the untrained student (0.733, CP@5 0.697) or the hard-label control (0.725, CP@5 0.704); `docs/reports/distillation.md` has the details. Its RAGAS rows weren't run: a reranker that loses 11% of retrieval can't pass the answer gate.
- **Both:** both of the above, and nothing new.

**Under load** (Locust, 50 users, 5 minutes, the same question mix; `reports/locust_50_ollama*` and `reports/locust_50_vllm*`):

| Generator | `/ask` requests | Failed | p50 | p95 | p99 | Throughput | GPU (median) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| q8 on Ollama | 125 | 40 (32%) | 66 s | 180 s | 180 s | 0.42/s | 99% |
| AWQ on vLLM | 242 | 4 (1.7%) | 49 s | 101 s | 162 s | 0.82/s | 99% |

Ollama generated one answer at a time (`OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL` 1). vLLM's continuous batching doubled throughput and cut failures from 32% to 1.7%. The GPU stayed saturated in both runs, now shared by vLLM and the `models` service. Retrieval runs one question at a time under the retriever's lock (Qdrant's local mode), and its rerank step costs 787 ms on an idle GPU.

**Decision.**
- **Generator: the 8-bit model stays.** AWQ fails the checklist's rule (faithfulness may drop by at most 0.03; it dropped 0.052) and falls below the 0.80 alert line. vLLM with AWQ stays available as `docker/compose.vllm.yml` for capacity, with its cost stated.
- **Reranker: the teacher stays.** The student is 6.1× faster at p95 (129 ms against 787 ms), but it loses 14 questions, and the CI gate tolerates one.

**Latency target (proposed; the owner to confirm).** `/ask` p95 ≤ 30 s at 50 concurrent users on this GPU, with under 1% failures. No configuration meets it: 180 s on Ollama, 101 s on vLLM.

**Stopping rule.** Stop a line of optimization when either holds:
1. Its next candidate breaks a quality gate: faithfulness down more than 0.03, or more than one retrieval question lost.
2. The component it speeds up is no longer on the critical path under load.

Both lines stop here by the first condition. The next lever isn't a cheaper model; it's the shared, serialized path:
- a Qdrant server, so retrieval needn't hold a lock;
- concurrent decoding for the 8-bit model (Ollama's `OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL`, or an 8-bit build under vLLM on a card with room for it), which keeps q8's faithfulness;
- the reranker on its own device.
